🔧 TROUBLESHOOTING
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
VM won't start:
  & "C:\Program Files\Oracle\VirtualBox\VBoxManage.exe" 
  startvm "AD-Server-Production"

Can't ping 192.168.1.50:
  - Check VM is running
  - Check network adapter is bridged
  - Try ipconfig in VM to verify IP

Domain join fails:
  - Verify DNS: nslookup meezan.local
  - Should return: 192.168.1.50
  - Check firewall in VM is off
  - Ping 192.168.1.50 from laptop

Scanner shows "Access Denied":
  - Use svc_scanner account (not Administrator)
  - Verify WinRM: Test-WSMan -ComputerName DC-NAME
